Good instinct to check this before building further. Honest assessment: the four features cover the real gaps in your original ask well — I wouldn't add more feature categories right now. But there are a couple of things that are commonly essential in restaurant billing (not scope creep, just gaps) that I'd flag before you call this "done":

Worth adding — genuine gaps, not creep:

Tax/GST on bills — none of the billing prompt mentions tax breakdown. In India, restaurant bills almost always need GST (or at least a tax line) shown separately from the subtotal. If your existing QR-order PDF bill already handles this, the manual billing flow needs to match it — otherwise you'll have two bill formats that don't agree, which looks unprofessional and could be a compliance issue.
Refund/void after payment — Billing currently covers create → discount → finalize → paid. There's no path for "customer already paid, but we need to void or partially refund this" (wrong item, complaint after payment, etc.). This is a normal POS scenario and worth a small addition, not a whole new feature.
Superadmin cross-outlet analytics view — Sales & Analytics is scoped per-outlet. If you run more than one outlet, Superadmin currently has no aggregate "all outlets" revenue view — only per-outlet drill-in. Might be fine if you're single-outlet for now, but flag it if you're not.

Resist adding for now — this is where scope creep starts:

Custom/dynamic role builder (your fixed 6 roles are enough until you have a concrete reason otherwise)
Split billing (you already deferred this correctly)
Loyalty programs, customer accounts, delivery integrations, table reservations — none of these were in your original ask, and each one is its own mini-system
Multi-currency, multi-language — not until you actually need them

My honest read: your instinct to stop at four is right. Add the tax/refund gaps (they're really part of "Billing" being complete, not new scope), skip everything else until real usage tells you it's needed.
